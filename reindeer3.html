CAD/Design 


 

  

This is just 3 of the many CAD files made for the reindeer. The general process was as follows: 

1. The Sculptor Mathew would create a sculpt of the element needed. For prototypes they were small, 
detatched versions. By the end they were full sized sculpts. 

2. 3D scan the sculpt and fix it blender.

3. Design the mechanism in Fusion 360 to work inside the sculpt.
